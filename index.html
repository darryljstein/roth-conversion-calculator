Social Security Spousal Modeling

When enabled, Client 2 keeps their own reduced benefit until Client 1 reaches filing/FRA age. At that point the model adds a reduced spousal excess using: own reduced benefit + ((50% × Client 1 PIA − Client 2 PIA) × reduction factor).
